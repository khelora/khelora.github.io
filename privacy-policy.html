Privacy Policy

Khelora games · Last updated: 3 October 2026

In short: our games do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details, and do not need an account. Your progress is saved on your own device. To improve our games, the games marked in the table below send anonymous gameplay statistics to our analytics provider, GameAnalytics. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

This policy applies to all mobile games published by Khelora ("we", "our games") that link to this page.

2. Information we do not collect

Our games do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files, and do not require you to create an account or sign in. Our games do not read your device's advertising identifier.

3. Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, a game saves information such as your coins, the items you have unlocked or equipped, your scores, your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls), daily reward progress, which tutorial tips you have seen, and which reminders and upgrade suggestions you have already been shown. This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

4. Analytics

Games marked with an analytics provider in the table above send anonymous usage statistics to that provider, so we can see how the game is played and make it better, for example which parts are too hard, how long a game lasts and whether players come back. For Split Ball this is GameAnalytics, which processes the data on our behalf. What is sent:

  • Gameplay events: when a game starts and ends, the score, how long it lasted, coins earned and spent, items bought or equipped, power-ups collected, daily rewards claimed, tutorial steps seen and settings changed.
  • Session information: when the game is opened and closed, and how long it was played.
  • Device information: device model and manufacturer, operating system version, game version and connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • An identifier: a random ID created by the analytics provider for this installation of the game. It is not your name, account or advertising ID, and it changes if you reinstall the game.
  • Country: your approximate country, worked out from your internet connection's IP address. We never see your IP address.
  • Crash and error reports: technical details when the game crashes, so we can fix it.

This information does not tell us who you are. It is sent over an encrypted connection, is used only to understand and improve our games, and is not sold or used for advertising. You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the game. To ask about, or ask us to delete, data sent from your device, contact us at the address below.

5. Information collected by the game engine

Our games are made with the Unity game engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information about your device (for example the device model, operating system version and graphics hardware) to improve its engine. This does not identify you. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

Games marked "None" in the table above show no advertising. If a game in the table shows advertising, the advertising partner named there may collect a device advertising identifier and basic device information to show and measure ads, as described in that partner's own privacy policy. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

7. In-app purchases

Games marked "None" in the table above have no in-app purchases; any in-game coins are earned only by playing. If a game offers purchases, payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

8. Permissions

A game only asks for the device permissions listed for it in the table above, and only for the purpose described. Internet and network state are used to send the analytics described above. Vibration is used for short vibrations during play and can be turned off in the game's settings.

Notifications: if you allow it, a game can remind you to come back when you have not played for a while (for example, that your daily reward is waiting). These reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else to do this, and opening the game cancels them. You can say no when the game asks, or turn notifications off at any time in your device's settings for the game. "Start after restart" only lets the game set those reminders again after your phone restarts; it does not run the game.

Ratings: a game may show Google Play's own "rate this game" window after a good moment. That window and any rating or review you leave are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive anything from it beyond the public review itself.

9. Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Sharing of information

We do not sell, trade or rent information about you. The analytics data described above is processed for us by the provider named in the table, under its own privacy policy, and is not shared with anyone else except where the law requires it.

11. Changes to this policy

If a game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising, this policy and the table above are updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or want to ask for your data to be deleted, contact us at abdur.dev89@gmail.com.
