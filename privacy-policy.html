Privacy Policy

Khelora games · Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: our games do not ask for, collect or share your personal information. Your progress is saved on your own device. The table below shows, for each game, whether it has advertising or in-app purchases.

This policy applies to all mobile games published by Khelora ("we", "our games") that link to this page.

2. Information we do not collect

Our games do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and do not require you to create an account or sign in.

3. Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, a game saves information such as your coins, the items you have unlocked or equipped, your scores, your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls), daily reward progress and which tutorial tips you have seen. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

4. Information collected by the game engine

Our games are made with the Unity game engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information about your device (for example the device model, operating system version and graphics hardware) to improve its engine. This does not identify you. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

Games marked "None" in the table above show no advertising. If a game in the table shows advertising, the advertising partner named there may collect a device advertising identifier and basic device information to show and measure ads, as described in that partner's own privacy policy. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

6. In-app purchases

Games marked "None" in the table above have no in-app purchases; any in-game coins are earned only by playing. If a game offers purchases, payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

7. Permissions

A game only asks for the device permissions listed for it in the table above, and only for the purpose described. Vibration is used for short vibrations during play and can be turned off in the game's settings.

8. Children's privacy

Our games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell, trade or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

10. Changes to this policy

If a game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising, this policy and the table above are updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at abdur.dev89@gmail.com.
